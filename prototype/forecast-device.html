The device itselfprocessor and memory, against 100%

The colour is a forecast, not a readingA port at 90% that has not moved in a month is grey here; a port at 40% climbing a point a day is red. The page is about what happens next, and a faceplate tinted by today's load would be the Traffic page wearing a costume.

Where it comes fromdt.statistics.forecast over the port's own daily peak, against the speed the port reports. In this prototype a least-squares fit with the standard error of its slope stands in for the analyzer.

Three states before any of thatA switch is mostly dark on purpose. Ports shut by an operator or with no module are drawn almost black, ports enabled and not up are outlined, and only what is actually carrying traffic can carry a forecast.
