Prototype · AheadForecast

Every number on this page is a forecastNothing here reports the present except as the last point of a series. The columns are the predicted value, the predicted date and the width of the band around both — a reading of today belongs on the Traffic page, not here.

Where the forecast comes fromThe platform's analyzer, dt.statistics.forecast, which returns the prediction and its bounds. The app propagates those bounds and never narrows them. On eob the endpoint answers 403 · davis:analyzers:read and the SDK client is installed: one scope. In this prototype a least-squares fit with its own standard error stands in for it, so the shapes are honest even though the engine is not the real one.

Where the limits come fromNever the app: the speed the port reports, the bandwidth the contract names, the hundred per cent of a processor.
