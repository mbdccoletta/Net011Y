Where the forecast comes fromThe platform's own analyzer, dt.statistics.forecast — not a regression written here, which would be the app inventing the judgement it refuses to make everywhere else. Checked on eob: the endpoint answers 403 · davis:analyzers:read, and the SDK client is already installed. One scope.

Where the limits come fromNever from the app. The speed the port reports (if.highspeed), the bandwidth the contract names (bandwidth_mbps), the hundred per cent of a processor. No threshold of its own, here either.

What it costsNothing new. Every series is a metric, and Grail bills logs, events and sessions. The rest of the app spends 31 GB of scan per load on this environment; this page can look back thirty days for none of it.
