Where the forecast comes from. Not from a regression written here — that would be the app inventing the judgement it refuses to make everywhere else. It asks the platform's own forecast analyzer, dt.statistics.forecast. Checked on eob today: the endpoint answers 403 · davis:analyzers:read, so it exists and the app simply lacks the scope. One line in app.config.json, approved at install.

What it costs. Nothing beyond what is already paid for. Every series this page reads is a metric, and Grail bills logs, events and sessions — not metrics. The rest of the app spends 14 to 32 GB of scan per load on this environment; this page can look back thirty days for none of it.

What the dates are not. A single day. The analyzer returns a band, and the page shows the band: "between 14 Feb and 2 Apr". A row whose band is wider than the horizon says so instead of naming a day nobody should plan against.
