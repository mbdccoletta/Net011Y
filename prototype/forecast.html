And in the pages you already use

The page above is for the question that spans the estate. The forecast of one subject belongs where that subject already lives — one strip, the same model, no new tab to remember.

Every number here is a forecastNothing reports the present except as the last point of a series. The dates are predictions and each carries the band around it; the confidence bars say how many days of record the prediction rests on.

Where it comes fromThe platform's analyzer, dt.statistics.forecast. On eob the endpoint answers 403 · davis:analyzers:read and the SDK client is installed: one scope. In this prototype a least-squares fit with the standard error of its slope stands in, so the shapes are honest even though the engine is not.

What it cannot do yetSeasonality. This customer's traffic follows the harvest, and a line fitted through thirty days of it will read a season as a trend. That needs a year of record; there are sixteen days. The page says so rather than pretending, and every date carries how much record is behind it.
